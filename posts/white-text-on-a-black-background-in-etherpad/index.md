[![](images/whiteonblack-550x191.png "whiteonblack")](images/whiteonblack.png)

Open /static/custom/pad.css and add:



```
#outerdocbody{ background-color:#000; }

#innerdocbody{ color:#fff; }
```



Save, close, hit refresh..
