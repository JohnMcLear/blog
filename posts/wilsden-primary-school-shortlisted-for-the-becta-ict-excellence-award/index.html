Wilsden Primary School shortlisted for the BECTA ICT Excellence award!

Wilsden Primary School has been shortlisted for an award for the fantastic work the parents, teachers and pupils have done this year with ICT.

The winners will be announced on the 19th of November.

Wilsden is up against 70 other schools in the UK.
